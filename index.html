THE VISION

Fewer windshield surveys.
Faster repairs.

Most municipalities still assess road condition the way they did fifty years ago: a small crew drives the network on a rotation, eyeballs the pavement, and writes down what they see. It's slow, it's subjective from one inspector to the next, and a network only gets looked at every few months — which means a crack that was cheap to seal in March is a pothole that's expensive to patch by June.

MetroVision·AI doesn't replace that judgment — it removes the bottleneck in front of it. Any phone photo, from any staff member, any resident report, or any routine drive-by, becomes an instant, consistent read on whether a stretch of road has a crack, a pothole, both, or nothing at all. The same square foot of pavement gets the same call whether it's inspected on a Monday morning or the last day of the fiscal year.

The result isn't a fancier report — it's more of the network getting looked at, more often, before small damage becomes an expensive repair. Maintenance budgets get spent on the streets that need it next, not just the ones with the loudest complaints.

Runs on your own infrastructure. No accounts, no cloud dependency, no resident data leaves your network.
